# Product notes: DOOH & Taxi Cab Advertising in India | Backseat Network

## What it is
India's self-serve in-cab advertising network that runs silent digital screens on headrest tablets in airport and city cabs across Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru and other Indian cities.

## Who it is for
Brands and agencies seeking to reach captive passengers in Indian cities, and fleet owners or drivers who can earn from referring campaigns.
