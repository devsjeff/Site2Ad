## In-cab advertising in India, explained

### What is in-cab advertising?

Backseat Network runs silent digital screens in airport cabs and city vehicles. Passengers see your brand on the tablet behind the driver. It is a form of digital out-of-home advertising, sometimes called cab digital out-of-home or CADOOH.

### Which Indian cities can I buy?

Mumbai, Delhi NCR and Bengaluru are live. Hyderabad, Chennai and Pune are onboarding, with more cities to follow. You pick the cab count and months in the console.

### How much does a campaign cost?

Media is ₹4,000 per cab per month. GST at 18% is added on the invoice. You pay online by UPI, card or netbanking through Razorpay when you submit the campaign, and the GST invoice is emailed automatically.

### How is a cab screen different from a hoarding or a digital billboard?

A hoarding or billboard is seen in passing. A cab screen sits at arm's length from one passenger for 20 to 45 minutes, every play is counted and tracked by GPS, and it carries a QR code the passenger can scan.

### Is there a minimum booking?

You can book from a single cab for one month (₹4,000 plus GST). Add cabs and months for more reach; the pricing calculator shows the total as you go.

### What creative do I need?

A silent landscape MP4 of about 15 seconds, or a JPG, PNG or WebP still, up to 40 MB, with a QR code on the creative. Vira can make it from your website. Every file is reviewed before it goes live.

### How do I know my ad played?

Each tablet reports every play of your creative, and your console shows the running totals and a live GPS map of the cabs carrying your campaign. QR scans show up in your own analytics through a tagged link.

### How do cab drivers join the network?

Drivers apply with their RC, driving licence and vehicle photos. Our team verifies the papers, a GPS installer mounts the company tablet, and campaigns then play silently for passengers.

### Can I refer a brand and earn from the sale?

Yes. Refer a brand, an agency or a local business, whether or not you already advertise with us. If they book, you earn 10% of the total sales on that deal. Send the introduction from the Refer and earn section on the homepage.
