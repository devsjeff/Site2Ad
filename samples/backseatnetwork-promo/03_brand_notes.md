# Brand notes: DOOH & Taxi Cab Advertising in India | Backseat Network

## Colours

- primary: **#ff6600** (text on it: #ffffff)
- accent: **#25d366**
- background (light): #faf8f5, (dark): #18181b
- text: #171717
- the site reads as a **light** design, so the video uses a light background

Where each colour was seen:

- #ff6600: button "Launch", button "Build your cab ad", large background area
- #ffffff: large background area, screenshots, link colour
- #f4efe8: large background area, screenshots
- #18181b: large background area, screenshots
- #f7f4ef: large background area, screenshots
- #efe8dc: large background area
- #242326: screenshots
- #e8e4dc: large background area

## Fonts

- headings: **Outfit** (weight 500, 60px)
- body: **Inter** (16px)

## Shapes

- pill-shaped buttons, rounded cards (24px), flat, shadowless surfaces

## Photo style

Realistic urban India scenes captured at eye level in moving cabs, featuring muted colours and neutral brightness showing passengers interacting with QR codes and tablet ads in Mumbai, Delhi and Bengaluru.

(measured from the screenshots: average brightness 0.67 (bright), neutral colour cast, muted)

## Tone of the copy

Direct and trustworthy, addressing busy professionals with short, factual sentences that emphasize results and ROI.

Tone words: professional, data-driven, reliable
